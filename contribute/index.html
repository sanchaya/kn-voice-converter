Help build an open Kannada speech-to-text model. Run by Sanchaya and Sanchi Foundation.
Draft consent text. It must be reviewed by a lawyer and a Kannada speaker before any public launch.

Your recordings, and the text you type or validate, will be stored on Sanchaya's own servers. They will be released publicly under the CC0 1.0 licence (no rights reserved) and used to train speech models. A voice can identify a person. Once a clip is in a published release it cannot be recalled; until then you can delete everything you gave, using the link at the bottom of this page.
